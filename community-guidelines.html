Community Guidelines

The practical rules that keep the Seek & Scale network useful and trustworthy.
Effective: August 30, 2026

Terms Privacy Community Guidelines

The simple rule

Be useful. Be real. Protect the room.

Seek & Scale works only if members trust the people and information inside it. These guidelines apply to profiles, posts, replies, PDFs, links, recommendations, opportunities, vouches, and member-to-member conduct.

1. Do not use the network as a cold-sales list

  • No bulk unsolicited pitching.
  • No copying member contact information into outside prospecting lists.
  • No repeated sales messages to members who did not ask for them.
  • No scraping the directory.

Relevant contact is fine. If someone posts an opportunity, asks for help, requests a referral, or there is a clear business reason to connect, reach out like a normal professional. Build relationships before trying to sell people.

2. Post things that belong in the room

Good posts include:

  • questions about running a service business;
  • useful lessons, wins, mistakes, or operational ideas;
  • real local business information;
  • legitimate work, referral, hiring, or talent opportunities;
  • PDFs, links, templates, articles, checklists, and tools that may help another owner;
  • genuine recommendations based on real experience.

A resource should include enough context for another member to understand why it is useful.

3. Keep opportunities real

  • Do not post fake work to generate leads.
  • Do not misrepresent compensation, timing, customer interest, or project details.
  • Close opportunities when they are no longer active.
  • Report outcomes and values honestly.
  • If you claim something and cannot follow through, release it quickly.

4. Recommendations and vouches must mean something

  • Recommend people or businesses because you actually have a basis for the recommendation.
  • Do not trade, buy, sell, or coordinate fake vouches.
  • Do not vouch for yourself through another account.
  • Do not manipulate trust signals.

5. Protect private information

Do not post customer names, addresses, phone numbers, payment information, passwords, private employee information, confidential proposals, private contracts, or other sensitive information unless you have a legitimate right and reason to share it.

Remove unnecessary personal information from screenshots, PDFs, estimates, invoices, and photos before posting them.

6. Respect people

  • No harassment, threats, bullying, stalking, or intimidation.
  • No discriminatory or hateful conduct.
  • No personal attacks disguised as “business feedback.”
  • Disagree with ideas without turning the room into a fight.

7. Do not post harmful or unlawful content

No fraud, illegal transactions, stolen material, malware, malicious links, intellectual-property infringement, deceptive impersonation, or content that violates applicable law.

8. Keep self-promotion proportional

You may talk about your business when it is relevant. You may share a win, explain what you do, respond to a request, or let the room know about something genuinely useful.

Do not turn every conversation into an advertisement. The Network should feel like useful owners talking to useful owners—not a room full of salespeople waiting to pitch each other.

9. Moderation is not a debate process

Seek & Scale may remove or limit any post, reply, resource, recommendation, opportunity, file, profile content, or other submission that we believe does not belong in the Network.

Seek & Scale may also restrict, suspend, or remove any member from the Network at any time, with or without notice, for any lawful reason or without stating a reason.

We may use warnings when they make sense, but a warning is not required before content or membership is removed.

10. When in doubt

Before posting, ask:

  • Is it true?
  • Is it useful to another local owner?
  • Do I have the right to share it?
  • Would I be comfortable saying it with the person standing next to me?
