Privacy Policy

How Seek & Scale handles information used to operate the member network.
Effective: August 30, 2026

Terms Privacy Community Guidelines

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Seek & Scale collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Network.

2. Information we collect

Account information

This may include your name, email address, authentication information, account status, invitation information, and the date/version of legal terms you accepted.

Member and business profile information

This may include your photo, business name, trade, city, headline, bio, phone number, website, social links, what your business does, how you can help members, and what you are looking for.

Content and activity

We collect Content you submit, including posts, replies, resources, PDFs, links, images, opportunities, recommendations, vouches, saved items, outcome information, reported opportunity value, introductions, and Get Help requests.

Technical and usage information

Our hosting, authentication, database, storage, security, and other service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, authentication events, error logs, and other information needed to operate and secure the Network.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage member accounts;
  • display member profiles and Network Content;
  • match members by trade, city, need, opportunity, or other relevance signals;
  • operate posts, replies, resource libraries, recommendations, saved items, and opportunities;
  • facilitate introductions and record whether connections or opportunities produced useful outcomes;
  • respond to Get Help requests;
  • send important account, activity, security, or Network communications;
  • create member digests or other re-engagement communications where enabled;
  • moderate the Network and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines;
  • protect members, prevent abuse, investigate misuse, and maintain security;
  • understand and improve Network usefulness, reliability, and performance;
  • comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.

4. What other members can see

Seek & Scale is a member network. Information you choose to put in your profile or post to the Network may be visible to other signed-in members.

Your profile includes privacy controls for certain contact information. You are responsible for deciding what business and personal information you are comfortable sharing with the Network.

Do not upload confidential customer or employee information just because the Network is private. Private membership does not make every document appropriate to share.

5. PDFs, images, and member-shared files

Member-shared PDFs and images may be stored using third-party cloud infrastructure and made available to signed-in members as needed to operate Network resource and content features.

Do not upload files containing sensitive personal information, confidential customer data, credentials, payment information, or information you do not have permission to share.

6. Service providers

We use third-party providers to help operate the Network. These may include providers for hosting, authentication, databases, file storage, code hosting, email, analytics, security, and other infrastructure.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to Seek & Scale and subject to their own agreements and legal obligations.

7. When we may share information

We may share information:

  • with other members when you intentionally make it visible in the Network;
  • with service providers that help us operate the Network;
  • when you ask us to facilitate an introduction, opportunity, or service;
  • when required by law, court order, subpoena, or lawful government request;
  • when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security threats, or violations of our rules;
  • to protect the rights, property, or safety of Seek & Scale, members, or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale, reorganization, or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to applicable law.

8. Sale of personal information

Seek & Scale does not operate the member directory as a data-broker product and does not sell member contact information to advertisers.

9. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the Network, maintain business and security records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with law, and preserve legitimate records of moderation, opportunities, outcomes, and transactions.

Deleted or removed information may remain for a period in backups, security logs, legal records, or moderation records.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security, and you use the Network with that understanding.

You are responsible for using a strong password and protecting access to your account and devices.

11. Your choices

You may update much of your profile directly in the Network and may control whether certain contact information is shown to members. You may also change available communication preferences where the feature is provided.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting Seek & Scale through the contact method provided to members. Requests may be limited where retention is required or permitted for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, contractual, or legitimate business reasons.

12. Children

Seek & Scale is intended for adults operating or working with businesses. The Network is not intended for children under 18.

13. Third-party websites

The Network may contain links to third-party websites and resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, not Seek & Scale.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Network, service providers, or legal requirements change. The version and effective date appear at the top of this page.

Questions about these documents may be sent through the contact method provided by Seek & Scale or through the in-app Get Help page.
